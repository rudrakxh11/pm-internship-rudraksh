# 1. My Understanding of Project Management Before Day 1

Before starting this internship, my understanding of Project Management was limited. I believed that a Project Manager's primary responsibility was assigning tasks to team members and monitoring whether the work was completed on time.

I was familiar with some technical aspects of software development, but I had very little knowledge about how projects are planned, documented, executed, monitored, and delivered. Concepts such as Business Requirements Documents (BRD), Product Requirements Documents (PRD), Stakeholder Management, Sprint Planning, RAID Logs, Change Requests, and Status Reporting were largely unfamiliar to me.

At that stage, I viewed project management as a coordination role rather than a structured discipline involving planning, communication, decision-making, risk management, and stakeholder alignment.

---

# 2. My Understanding of Project Management After Day 20

After completing this internship, my understanding of Project Management has significantly improved.

I now understand that project management is a structured process that begins with identifying business needs and continues through planning, execution, monitoring, and project closure.

Throughout this internship, I learned how to:

* Define business goals using a BRD.
* Translate business requirements into product features through a PRD.
* Identify and manage stakeholders.
* Create communication plans.
* Plan and manage Agile sprints.
* Track project risks using Risk Registers and RAID Logs.
* Handle project changes through Change Requests.
* Monitor project health using status reports and RAG status.
* Conduct sprint reviews and retrospectives.
* Maintain project documentation throughout the project lifecycle.

I now recognize that successful project delivery depends not only on technical execution but also on communication, planning, stakeholder management, and continuous monitoring.

---

# 3. Most Important Learning

The most important lesson I learned during this internship is the importance of clear requirements and stakeholder alignment.

Many project challenges originate from unclear requirements, changing expectations, or poor communication between stakeholders and project teams.

By documenting requirements properly, maintaining regular communication, and reviewing progress continuously, a Project Manager can significantly reduce risks and improve project outcomes.

This understanding has changed the way I view both software development and project execution.
